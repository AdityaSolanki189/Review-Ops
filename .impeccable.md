## Design Context

### Users
Azzurro Sydney hotel managers scanning reviews between shifts, often on a laptop, sometimes on a phone.

### Brand Personality
Calm, operational, coastal. Not a marketing site. Confidence through clarity, not decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction
Internal ops dashboard using shadcn/ui new-york. Geist Sans for UI, Geist Mono for ratings and timestamps. Azure-tinted neutrals (hue ~240), one accent. Light and dark themes, default system.

### Design Principles
1. Answer operational questions first: which property slipped, what topics hurt, what the guest said.
2. Dense but readable: fixed type scale, generous touch targets, no decorative motion on daily screens.
3. Token-driven surfaces only; no ad-hoc hex or AI-purple gradients.
4. Progressive disclosure: scannable lists, full detail in sheets.
5. Fast perceived load: independent section skeletons, server prefetch where possible.
